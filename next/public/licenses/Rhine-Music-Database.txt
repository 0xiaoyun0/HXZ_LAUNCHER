Rhine Music Database
https://github.com/MikeQu223/Rhine-Music-Database
Source revision: 36aa998228f63d7ab5d43484102aaccbccf7fb02

The music archive presentation adapts the upstream HUD, music browser, detail and playback interface. Scene models and motion are reused through the upstream RhineLabUI Web engine with an isolated song catalog. The launcher retains its own audio, account and network services. Qt, upstream account/network code, fonts and audio are not bundled. See RhineLabUI-MIT.txt for the scene and model notice.
